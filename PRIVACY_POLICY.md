# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** June 5, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Worktrax ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide
- **Account Data:** Email address and password (if you sign up with email) or Google account information (if you sign in with Google)
- **Profile Data:** Your chosen display name
- **Workout Data:** Exercises, sets, reps, weight, duration, distance, RPE, rest times, and workout timestamps
- **Body Metrics:** Bodyweight entries and body measurements (chest, waist, hips, arm, thigh, calf)
- **Routines:** Saved workout routines you create

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Usage Data:** Firebase Analytics collects anonymous usage events (workout completions, feature usage, theme preferences) to help us improve the app
- **Device Info:** Device model, OS version, app version (via Firebase Analytics)

## 3. How We Use Your Information

- To provide and maintain the workout tracking service
- To sync your data across devices via Firebase (optional, only if you sign in)
- To send you optional workout reminders (you can enable/disable in Settings)
- To improve the app based on aggregated usage analytics
- To notify you of volume milestones (you've lifted 1,000 kg, etc.)

## 4. Data Storage and Security

- **Local Storage:** Your primary data is stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences
- **Cloud Storage:** If you sign in, your data is synced to Firebase Realtime Database (Google Cloud). All data is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS
- **Authentication:** Firebase Authentication handles sign-in securely; we never store your password
- **Data Retention:** We retain your cloud data until you delete your account

## 5. Third-Party Services

We use the following Google services:

| Service | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| Firebase Authentication | User sign-in (email/password and Google) |
| Firebase Realtime Database | Cloud data sync |
| Firebase Analytics | Anonymous usage analytics |
| Google Sign-In | Optional sign-in method |

These services are governed by Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 6. Your Rights

You can:
- **Access** your data at any time within the app
- **Export** your data via PDF report or CSV download
- **Delete** your account and all associated data via Settings → Delete Account. This removes your data from Firebase Auth and Realtime Database
- **Opt out** of analytics by disabling Firebase Analytics (device-level setting)
- **Control notifications** via Settings → Workout Reminder toggle

## 7. Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted here with an updated "Last updated" date.
